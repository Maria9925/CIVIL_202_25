Question 1 (***)[R]#

Water flows through a sluice gate as shown below. The friction losses through the gate are estimated to be 0.2 m. Find the horizontal thrust of the water per meter width on the sluice gate. Given: y1 = 2.2 m, y2 = 0.4 m, y3 = 0.5 m

Question 2 (***)#

Flow occurs over a spillway with a width of 100 m. The spillway has a cross section as shown below. The water depth on the upstream side of the spillway (y1) is 20 m, and on the downstream side (y2) is 1.5 m. The friction energy headloss over the spillway is 1.2 m. Determine the horizontal force of the water on the spillway.

Answer

\( 147.7 \; MN\; to \; the \; right \)

Hint

Apply the momentum equation in the horizontal direction.

Find the velocities using the Energy equation.

Apply Newton’s Second Law to find the horizontal force.

Solution

Show code cell outputs

Conservation of mass:

\[\begin{split} Q_1 &= Q_2 \\ v_1 A_1 &= v_2 A_2 \\ v_1 y_1 b &= v_2 y_2 b \\ v_2 &= \frac{y_1 v_1}{y_2} \end{split}\]

Conservation of Energy:

\[\begin{split} z_1 + \frac{P_1}{\rho g} + \frac{v_1^2}{2g} &= z_2 + \frac{P_2}{\rho g} + \frac{v_2^2}{2g} + h_f \\ \frac{P_1}{\rho g} &= y_1, \quad \frac{P_2}{\rho g} = y_2\\ z_1 = z_2\\ \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} \frac{v_1^2}{2g} &= y_2 + \frac{v_2^2}{2g} + h_f - y_1 \\ \frac{v_1^2}{2g} &= y_2 + \frac{y_1^2 v_1^2}{2g y_2^2} + h_f - y_1 \end{split}\]

Once the velocities at both sections are determined, the conservation of momentum can then be applied:

\[\begin{split} \sum F_x &= \rho Q (v_2 - v_1) \\ F_1 - F_2 -R_x &= \rho Q (v_2 - v_1) \end{split}\]

The centroid of the spillway is:

\[ \rho g \frac{y_1}{2} A_1 - \rho g \frac{y_2}{2} A_2 - F &= \rho Q (v_2 - v_1) \]
Show code cell outputs Hide code cell outputs
\[\begin{split} \begin{aligned} b &= 100 \; \;\textrm{(m)} \\[10pt] y_{1} &= 20 \; \;\textrm{(m)} \\[10pt] y_{2} &= 1.500 \; \;\textrm{(m)} \\[10pt] h_{f} &= 1.200 \; \;\textrm{(m)} \\[10pt] v_{1} &= \left( \frac{ \left( y_{1} - y_{2} - h_{f} \right) \cdot 2 \cdot g }{ \left( \frac{ y_{1} }{ y_{2} } \right) ^{ 2 } - 1 } \right) ^{ 0.5 } \\&= \left( \frac{ \left( 20 - 1.500 - 1.200 \right) \cdot 2 \cdot 9.810 }{ \left( \frac{ 20 }{ 1.500 } \right) ^{ 2 } - 1 } \right) ^{ 0.5 } \\&= 1.386 \; \;\textrm{(m/s)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] v_{2} &= y_{1} \cdot \frac{ v_{1} }{ y_{2} } \\&= 20 \cdot \frac{ 1.386 }{ 1.500 } \\&= 18.476 \; \;\textrm{(m/s)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] Q &= v_{1} \cdot y_{1} \cdot b \\&= 1.386 \cdot 20 \cdot 100 \\&= 2771.333 \; \;\textrm{(m3/s)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] A_{1} &= b \cdot y_{1} \\&= 100 \cdot 20 \\&= 2000 \; \;\textrm{(m2)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] A_{2} &= b \cdot y_{2} \\&= 100 \cdot 1.500 \\&= 150.000 \; \;\textrm{(m2)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] R_{x} &= \rho_{water} \cdot g \cdot y_{1} \cdot \frac{ A_{1} }{ 2 } - \rho_{water} \cdot g \cdot y_{2} \cdot \frac{ A_{2} }{ 2 } - \rho_{water} \cdot Q \cdot \left( v_{2} - v_{1} \right) \\&= 1000 \cdot 9.810 \cdot 20 \cdot \frac{ 2000 }{ 2 } - 1000 \cdot 9.810 \cdot 1.500 \cdot \frac{ 150.000 }{ 2 } - 1000 \cdot 2771.333 \cdot \left( 18.476 - 1.386 \right) \\&= 147.735 \; \;\textrm{(N)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] R_{x} &= \frac{ R_{x} }{ 1000000 } \\&= \frac{ 147.735 }{ 1000000 } \\&= 147.735 \; \;\textrm{(MN)}\\[10pt] \end{aligned} \end{split}\]
Show code cell outputs

The force on the structure is on the same site but opposite in direction (\(N \mathrm{III}\)）: \(F_x = 147.735 MN\) to the right.

Question 2 (**)#

The diameters in the figure below are 750 mm and 500 mm. The pressure at point 1 is 650 kPa and the velocity 2.8 m/s. Neglecting friction, find the resultant force on the horizontal conical reducer if water flows:

a. to the right.

b. to the left.

c. What would happen with the previous answers if we did not neglect friction?

Answer

\( a) \;158.3 \; kN\;to\;the\;right \)

\( b) \;158.3 \; kN\;to\;the\;right \)

\( c) \;increase \; Fx\;in\;(a)\;and\;decrease\;Fx\;in\;(b) \)

Hint

Apply the conservation of mass to relate the flow rates at different sections and solve for the unknow velocity.

Apply the energy equation.

Apply \(N \mathrm{II}\) in the horizontal direction to calculate the force on the reducer.

Question 2 (a)

Solution

Show code cell outputs

Conservation of mass:

\[\begin{split} Q_1=Q_2\\ v_1A_1=v_2A_2\\ v_2=\frac{v_1A_1}{A_2} \end{split}\]

Conservation of energy:

\[\begin{split} z_1+\frac {P_1}{\rho g}+\frac{v_1^2}{2g}=z_2+\frac {P_2}{\rho g}+\frac{v_2^2}{2g}\\ z_1= 0, z_2=0\\ \frac {P_1}{\rho g}+\frac{v_1^2}{2g}=\frac {P_2}{\rho g}+\frac{v_2^2}{2g}\\ P_2=(\frac {P_1}{\rho g}+\frac{v_1^2}{2g}-\frac{v_2^2}{2g}) \rho g \end{split}\]

Conservation of momentum:

\[\begin{split} \sum F_x = \rho Q (v_2-v_1)\\ P_1A_1-P_2A_2-R_x = \rho Q (v_2-v_1)\\ \end{split}\]

\[\begin{split} \begin{aligned} D_{1} &= \frac{ 750 }{ 1000 } &= 0.750 \; \;\textrm{(m)} \\[10pt] D_{2} &= \frac{ 500 }{ 1000 } &= 0.500 \; \;\textrm{(m)} \\[10pt] p_{1} &= 650 \cdot 1000 &= 650000 \; \;\textrm{(Pa)} \\[10pt] v_{1} &= 2.800 \; \;\textrm{(m/s)} \\[10pt] v_{2} &= \frac{ v_{1} \cdot \left( \pi \cdot \frac{ \left( D_{1} \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 4 } \right) }{ \pi \cdot \frac{ \left( D_{2} \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 4 } } \\&= \frac{ 2.800 \cdot \left( 3.142 \cdot \frac{ \left( 0.750 \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 4 } \right) }{ 3.142 \cdot \frac{ \left( 0.500 \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 4 } } \\&= 6.300 \; \;\textrm{(m/s)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] p_{2} &= \left( \left( \frac{ p_{1} }{ \rho_{water} \cdot g } \right) + \left( \frac{ \left( v_{1} \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 2 \cdot g } \right) - \left( \frac{ \left( v_{2} \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 2 \cdot g } \right) \right) \cdot \rho_{water} \cdot g \\&= \left( \left( \frac{ 650000 }{ 1000 \cdot 9.810 } \right) + \left( \frac{ \left( 2.800 \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 2 \cdot 9.810 } \right) - \left( \frac{ \left( 6.300 \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 2 \cdot 9.810 } \right) \right) \cdot 1000 \cdot 9.810 \\&= 634075.000 \; \;\textrm{(Pa)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] A_{1} &= \pi \cdot \frac{ \left( D_{1} \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 4 } \\&= 3.142 \cdot \frac{ \left( 0.750 \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 4 } \\&= 0.442 \; \;\textrm{(m2)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] A_{2} &= \pi \cdot \frac{ \left( D_{2} \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 4 } \\&= 3.142 \cdot \frac{ \left( 0.500 \right) ^{ 2 } }{ 4 } \\&= 0.196 \; \;\textrm{(m2)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] Q &= v_{1} \cdot A_{1} \\&= 2.800 \cdot 0.442 \\&= 1.237 \; \;\textrm{(m3/s)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] R_{x} &= p_{1} \cdot A_{1} - p_{2} \cdot A_{2} - \rho_{water} \cdot Q \cdot \left( v_{2} - v_{1} \right) \\&= 650000 \cdot 0.442 - 634075.000 \cdot 0.196 - 1000 \cdot 1.237 \cdot \left( 6.300 - 2.800 \right) \\&= 158331.361 \; \;\textrm{(N)}\\[10pt] \\[10pt] R_{x_{}} &= \frac{ R_{x} }{ 1000 } \\&= \frac{ 158331.361 }{ 1000 } \\&= 158.331 \; \;\textrm{(kN)}\\[10pt] \end{aligned} \end{split}\]
