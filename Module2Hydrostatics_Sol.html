a) Express the pressure as an absolute pressure measured in Pa.

c) Express the pressure as a absolute pressure head of mercury (assume the density of mercury to be 13 600 kg/m3).

\( b) \; 9.89 \; m \)

\( c) \; 0.73 \; m \)

Question 1 (a)

Hint

mbar means millibar

1 bar = 100 kPa
